Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

YouTube API Services

Night Noodle Scheduler uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your use of YouTube data is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access and collect

When you connect YouTube, the app receives an OAuth access token and refresh token from Google, and reads your channel name (youtube.readonly) to show which channel is connected. It uploads videos and thumbnails you choose (youtube.upload). When you connect TikTok, it receives tokens and your display name. It also stores the videos, captions and schedule you enter.

How we use it

Only to publish your own videos to your own accounts at the times you choose, and to show the connected account name.

Where it is stored

Only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's and TikTok's official APIs. No cookies, analytics or tracking.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose your data to any third party.

Deleting your data

Click the connected account in the app to disconnect it; this immediately deletes its stored tokens. Deleting a post in the app deletes its video file. You can also revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings or in your TikTok app settings. Stored tokens are deleted within 30 days of revocation if not already removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/Bswizzy/night-noodle-scheduler.
