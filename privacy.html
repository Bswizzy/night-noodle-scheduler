Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

What we collect: when you connect an account, the app stores the access tokens and basic profile name that TikTok or Google return, plus the videos, captions and schedule you enter.

Where it's stored: only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's and Google's official APIs to publish your posts.

Sharing: no data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deletion: disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens. You can also revoke access any time in your TikTok or Google account settings.
